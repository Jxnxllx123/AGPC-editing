Who We Are

We are a Presbyterian church in Singapore, a Christ-centered community committed to worship, discipleship, and mission.

We affirm the historic Christian faith expressed in the Apostles’ Creed and Reformed tradition.

Member church of The Presbyterian Church in Singapore

OUR PURPOSE

Vision, Mission & Values

The vision that guides us, the mission that sends us, and the values that shape our life together.

01 — OUR DIRECTION

Our Vision

To establish a church that glorifies God, to worship God with sincerity and truth.

Teaching truth based on the Bible, and fellowship with one another through love and mutual assistance.

Care for the community with love and compassion, and spread the gospel through preaching and life.

02 — OUR CALLING

Our Mission

To become disciples of all nations.

03 — OUR CHARACTER

Our Values

Loyalty, Faith, Love, Unity, and Perseverance.
