Abundant Grace Student Care

Abundant Grace Student Care Centre offers supportive care and engaging programmes for children outside school hours.

Our activities include practical learning experiences like sign language training during holidays, cookery sessions, fun cultural lessons during Chinese New Year, and creative holiday programmes designed to help children learn by doing.

Programme Details
  • Level: Primary 1 – Primary 6
  • Operating Days: Monday to Friday
  • Time: 1:30pm – 6:30pm

Music Ministry

At AGPC, the Music Ministry is divided into Worship Music and Music Education.

Worship music is led by the worship team, choir, and pianists. During different seasons, we aim to enrich worship through artistic offerings. Music education helps children and adults develop skills, serve, and fellowship together.

Through the Music Ministry, we hope more people will join in singing: 'To Him who sits on the throne and to the Lamb be praise, honor, glory, and power forever and ever.'

  • Adult Choir Practice: Sunday · 1:00–3:00pm
  • Worship Team Practice: Saturday · 2:00–4:00pm

Outreach & Missions

Missions is the heartbeat of the church. The church exists to proclaim the gospel, and continues to thrive through missions. As followers of Christ, we are called to emulate Him by entering the world and spreading God’s kingdom. This is not optional—every Christian is called to participate and dedicate themselves to the work of missions.

Abroad, we support missionaries and ministries in Cambodia, Australia, Thailand, and Myanmar. Domestically, we have ministered to Chinese migrant workers since 2009, leading to evening gatherings.

In community care, we operate a Student Care Centre and support local gospel organizations such as the Widow’s Love Association, Global Communication, and ORTV. The Evangelism Committee leads local gospel outreach, organizes evangelistic events (e.g., Parents’ Day, Mid-Autumn, Christmas), and trains volunteers to support newcomers in Sunday services.
